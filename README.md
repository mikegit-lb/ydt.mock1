# YDT Master — Advanced English Grammar & Cloze Test Prep (ÖSYM C1/C2)

A modern, distraction-free Single-Page Application tailored for students preparing for the Turkish **YDT (Yükseköğretim Kurumları Sınavı – Yabancı Dil Testi)** and **YDS** English exams.

## Key Features

- **100 High-Difficulty Questions (C1/C2 Academic Standard)**:
  - **50 Standalone Grammar MCQs** balanced across 7 core ÖSYM topics:
    - *Tenses & Time Adverbials* (8)
    - *Modals & Semi-Modals* (6)
    - *Passives, Causatives & Participial Reductions* (8)
    - *Conjunctions, Transitions & Adverbial Clauses* (10)
    - *Relative & Noun Clauses* (6)
    - *Prepositions & Phrasal Verbs* (6)
    - *Inversion & Conditionals* (6)
  - **50 Cloze Test Questions (10 Academic Passages × 5 Blanks)** across History, Nature & Environment, Scientific Discoveries, Biographies, Psychology & Neuroscience, and Space & Astrophysics.
  - **Bilingual Explanations (TR + EN)**: Every question includes grammar cues, full Turkish sentence/passage translations, Turkish & English rule breakdowns, and option-by-option distractor analyses.
- **Dual Study Modes**:
  - **Practice Mode**: Instant color-coded feedback (`emerald` / `rose`) and automatic expansion of the bilingual Rule Analysis card.
  - **Mock Exam Mode**: Live timer and official **ÖSYM Net Score Card** ($\text{Net} = \text{Correct} - \frac{\text{Incorrect}}{4}$) with topic-by-topic performance bars and post-exam solution review.
- **Built-In Interactive Study & Annotation Toolkit**:
  - **Highlight Text (`H`)** & **Underline Text (`U`)** directly on words in question stems and Cloze passages.
  - **Freehand Pen (`P`)**, **Straight Rule Line (`L`)**, **Freehand Marker (`M`)**, and **Eraser (`X`)** canvas overlay with 5 color swatches and Undo (`Ctrl+Z`).
  - **Distractor Elimination (`/` button or right-click)** to cross out eliminated options (`A`–`E`).
- **Full `localStorage` Persistence**: Saves solved questions, bookmarks (`★`), annotations, dark/light theme preference, and Mock Exam score history.

## Getting Started

npm install
